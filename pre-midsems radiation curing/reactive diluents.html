what will be in a UV curing?

  • reactive diluent
  • additive
  • photosensitive material

reactive diluent

Oligomers
  • monomers linked through covalent bonds
  • 3000-7000 MW
  • fewer monomers
  1. shearing action -i.e. Change the surface tension
    • ST is necessary to wet the surface
    • hence better retention
  2. altering functionality and reactivity
    • vinyl groups
Functionality

  1. reduced viscosity
    • SOLVENTS - true solvent (completely compatible), diluent (some affinity), non solvent (no compatibility)
    • diluent - no VOCs
  2. improvement in chemical resistance
  3. change the physical appearance
    • gloss - how non-distorted an image appears on the coating surface
  4. Alters flexibility ( eg coil coating)
  5. Alters impact properties (automobiles) - higher flexibility -> longer chain molecules polymers
  6. improving abrasion resistance (higher hardness, higher abrasion resistance)

  • Viscosity

  • : glass transition temperature
  • skin irritation

Methacrylate
  • absorbs UV radiation

chain termination is any chemical reaction that ceases the formation of reactive intermediates in a chain propagation step in the course of a polymerization, effectively bringing it to a halt.

functionality

  • mono-functional : one vinyl group
  • di
  • tri
  • tetra

mono functional groups

  • less in size, hence works as a solvent - higher tendency to dissolve the oligomers
  • higher cutting power - lessens viscosity
  • low reactivity
  • lower curing speed
  • high volatility
  • strong odor
  • skin irritation
  • flammability

di-functional reactive diluent

  • 2 vinyl groups
  • lowest viscosity
  • better cutting power
  • better reactivity
  • faster curing
  • good hardness
1,4 butanediol diacrylate

1,4 Butanediol +acrylate -> 1,4 butanediol diacrylate

  • methyl group - higher hence rigid structure
  • ether linkages - less
  • neopentyl glycol diacrylate - higher scratch resistance, higher chemical resistance
  • TEGlycol dimethacrylate - flexible

tri-functional

  • higher reactivity

how the physico-chemical properties come into play

  • ether linkages - flexible
  • color - gardener scale
  • moisture content - less gloss, water is highly non-compatible with the coating
  • refractive index - opacity
  • - benzene group will increase the
  • viscosity - cutting power
  • ester linkages - less flexible

structure property relation

shrinkage

  • directly related to the % conversion of double bounds
  • more functionality, higher shrinkage
  • depends on mol. wt.
  • acrylic coating the % shrinkage varies from 5 to 10 %
  • coating adhesion will be better on porous substrate than non-porous
  • it will lead to negative entropy and less free volume
  • find the formula for max shrinkage%

reactivity

  • Functionality and reactivity are closely related.
  • Higher the functionality higher is the crosslinking density.
  • Higher will be tensile strength but lower will be elongation.

surface tension

st is imp for wetting

  • methacr monomer has lower surface tension
  • etholyxation decreases
  • propoxylation decreases
  • acrylic acid and acrylamide monomers enhance wetting
  • residual hydroxyl enhances adhesion on metallic substrate

refractive index

  • related with gloss
  • higher Ri, higher gloss, higher gloss higher clarity
  • as MW increases, RI increases (which is why methacr are glossier)

volatility, odor, flammability and toxicity

  • higher volatility, higher odor (exceptions - IBOMA and NVP)
  • octyl and iso decyl -> non flammable
  • ethoxy ethoxy ethyl acr mild odor, high irritancy
  • NPGDA is carcinogenic
  • propoxylated NPGDA is less toxic and irritant

weatheribility and color stability

  • methacr better weatheribility
  • isoboronyl acrylate good weather resistance
  • 1,6 hexane diol better weather resistance
  • hydrophilic reactive diluents are inferior in weatheribility
  • It depends on the structure of the polymer backbone
  • Alipahtic/ aromatic
    • 1,6 Hexanediol exhibit excellent weather resistance
  • Hydrophilic/ hydrophobic
    • Hydrophilic reactive diluents are inferior to weatherability
    • Isoboronyl acrylate is exceptionally good in weather resistance

polarity

  • heteroatoms polar behaviour - hydrophillic
  • hydroxy is also hydrophillic
  • fluoro is hydrophobic
  • acr more polar than methacr
  • hydrophillic monomers - resistant to polar solvents (water, acetone)
  • hydrophobic monomers - resistant to non-polar solvents (benzene, xylene)

selection of rd

in case of reactive diluent the selection will depend on

  • Viscosity
  • Reactivity
  • Functionality
  • Compatibility with oligomers
  • Skin irritancy
