5. Gasoline and diesel

5.1. gasoline

  • volatile fuel in spark ignition
  • BP range - 30- 200 ° C
  • mixture of HCs from to
  • BIS, IS 2679 specification
Note

AKI stands for anti-knocking index

5.1.1. basic requirements

  • burn smoothly without knock
  • be sufficiently volatile to give flammable mixture under all operating conditions
  • good fuel economy

5.1.2. compositions

  • less cyclic alkanes and alkenes
  • additives

5.1.3. characterstics

  1. density
  2. vapor pressure
  3. distillation
  4. octane number
  5. Cu strip corrosion (helps in detecting the acid components)
  6. existent gum
  7. benzene content
  8. V/L ratio
  9. sulphur
  10. olefins content
  11. aromatic content

5.2. diesel

5.2.1. characterstics of diesel

  1. cloud point
  2. pour point
  3. flash point
  4. acidity
  5. viscosity
  6. distillation
  7. sulphur
  8. ash
  9. water content
  10. carbon residue
  11. cetane number, PAH
Note
  • furnace oil, coke -> optimisation
  • blend -> not total mixing (understand the process, priced higher/less)

6. problems in blending

  1. wild streams sent to product tank
  2. quality change during blending - no online analysis
  3. longer lab analysis - giveaway reactions
  4. component ratio is difficult to control
